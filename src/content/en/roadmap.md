---
title: Roadmap
description: Roadmap of upcoming features for fancanon
subtitle: Roadmap of upcoming features for fancanon. (No we will not be in beta forever.)
step: 6
steps:
  - step: 1
    title: Modern Reading Experience
    description: A beautiful, distraction-free reading experience with polished
      typography, smooth animations, responsive layouts, and thoughtful
      quality-of-life improvements across the site.
  - step: 2
    title: Read Later & Bookmarks
    description: Save stories for later, bookmark your favorite chapters, and easily
      jump back to exactly where you left off.
  - step: 3
    title: Comments
    description: Leave feedback on chapters, discuss your favorite moments, and
      connect with authors and fellow readers.
  - step: 4
    title: Narration
    description: Listen to stories with built-in text-to-speech narration, making it
      easy to enjoy fanfiction while commuting, relaxing, or multitasking.
  - step: 5
    title: Feed & Social Features
    description: Follow your favorite writers, like stories, and keep up with the latest activity from
      the community.
  - step: 6
    title: Notifications
    description: Never miss a chapter update, comment, reply, like, or new story
      from the authors and fandoms you follow.
  - step: 7
    title: Music Pairing
    description: Authors can attach Spotify playlists to individual chapters,
      letting readers experience the soundtrack that inspired each scene.
  - step: 8
    title: History & Analytics
    description: Track your reading history and gain insights into story performance
      with detailed reader analytics.
  - step: 9
    title: Curated Collections
    description: Browse handpicked collections of exceptional stories, themed
      recommendations, seasonal events, and hidden gems curated by the
      community.
  - step: 10
    title: Improved Search
    description: Find exactly what you're looking for with powerful filtering by
      fandom, characters, relationships, tags, ratings, completion status, and
      more.
  - step: 11
    title: User Submitted Narration
    description: Community members can contribute their own narrated versions of
      stories, giving readers multiple voices and styles to choose from.
---
